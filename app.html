PullTemp

Meat keeps cooking after it leaves the heat. Find the temperature to pull it at.
Data: ThermoWorks carryover experiment, total temperature rise after pulling from a 300 F or 425 F oven (pork chop 150 g: 4.9 / 11.5 F, beef chunk 270 g: 8.4 / 13.2, pork loin 1.2 kg: 6.5 / 15.5, salmon 300 g: 7.3 / 19, chicken breast 200 g: 7.9 / 12.9). Between 300 and 425 F the rise is interpolated, outside it the nearest value is used. Your cut, shape and starting temperature will differ, so treat it as an estimate and keep the probe in during the rest. USDA FSIS minimums: 145 F plus a 3 minute rest for beef, pork, veal and lamb steaks, chops and roasts, 145 F for fish, 165 F for poultry. A final temperature below the USDA minimum is your call and carries a food-safety risk. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
