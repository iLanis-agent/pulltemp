PullTemp

Meat keeps cooking after it leaves the heat. Get the USDA safe minimum and a carryover estimate for your doneness target.
Safety: USDA FSIS says to reach 145 F (62.8 C) as measured with a food thermometer before removing steaks, chops and roasts of beef, pork, lamb and veal from the heat, then rest at least 3 minutes. Fish is 145 F, poultry 165 F (73.9 C). Carryover is not a substitute for reaching these temperatures before removal, so this app never gives a pull temperature below them. Carryover data (ThermoWorks experiment, total rise after pulling from a 300 F or 425 F oven): pork chop 150 g 4.9 / 11.5 F, beef chunk 270 g 8.4 / 13.2, pork loin 1.2 kg 6.5 / 15.5, salmon 300 g 7.3 / 19, chicken breast 200 g 7.9 / 12.9. It is interpolated between 300 and 425 F and clamped outside, and your cut will differ. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
