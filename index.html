PullTemp
The thermometer should read lower when you pull the meat than when you eat it.
Open the app
A pork chop from a 425 F oven keeps climbing about 11.5 F after you pull it. In a 300 F oven it climbs 4.9 F.

What it does

  • Pull temperature for chops, roasts, fish and chicken
  • Carryover from measured data at 300 F and 425 F, interpolated between
  • F and C, with USDA minimum and rest warnings
  • Tells you to keep the probe in during the rest
Carryover data from ThermoWorks experiments, safe temperatures from USDA FSIS. An estimate: your cut and shape will differ. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
