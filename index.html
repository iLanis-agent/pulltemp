PullTemp
Meat keeps cooking after you pull it. Safety comes first.
Open the app
USDA: reach 145 F on a thermometer before you take a pork chop off the heat, then rest it 3 minutes. Carryover can add 5 to 12 F more.

What it does

  • USDA safe minimum, to reach before the food leaves the heat
  • A carryover estimate from measured data, for your doneness target
  • Never a pull temperature below the safe minimum
  • F and C, chops, roasts, fish and chicken
Safe temperatures from USDA FSIS. Carryover estimates from ThermoWorks experiments: your cut and shape will differ. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
